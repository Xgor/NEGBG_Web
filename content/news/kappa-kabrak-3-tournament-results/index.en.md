Another Kabrak is now behind us. On Sunday, May 31, we once again held a tournament at Kappa Bar, this time featuring Street Fighter 6. Some of our crew couldn’t make it, but we still had 10 Street Fighters eager for victory!
Some of us were already a bit worn out from the Fightbox event in Trollhättan that several of us attended the day before, but in Gothenburg there always seems to be an appetite for more Street Fighter! 

Congratulations to Furbylon who took home the victory! And also to TheArpeggiator and Jenxey who took 2nd and 3rd place, respectively.

Thanks to everyone who participated and watched.
See you next time!
