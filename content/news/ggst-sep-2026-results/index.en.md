On Friday September 11 it was finally time again: after the summer break we kicked off the second edition of Goa Gubbars SlagsmålsTurnering at Kappa Bar. 14 players, double elimination, a stream running all night, and a grand final where both sets came down to the last game.

<!--more-->

{{< figure src="gruppbild.jpg" caption="Group photo outside Kappa before we got started. Thanks to everyone who showed up!" >}}

It was good to see that interest in GGST didn't cool off over the summer. If anything we got both familiar faces and a few new ones who weren't shy about pushing hard from the very first match.

{{< figure src="publiken.jpg" caption="Kappa Bar filled up as the evening went on" >}}

Between sets there was as much hanging out as there was playing. People gathered around the tables, talked matchups and kept an eye on whatever was running on the screens.

{{< figure src="stream.jpg" caption="The commentary booth reacting to something that probably should not have worked" >}}

The stream was run from a corner of the bar all night, and the commentators had no trouble finding energy once the matches started running long.

{{< figure src="bracket.jpg" alt="The bracket on the big screen at Kappa Bar" caption="The bracket up on the big screen above the bar" >}}

The bracket stayed up on the big screen all evening, which made it easy to keep track of who was on deck and who had already been sent down to losers.

{{< figure src="publiken2.jpg" caption="The crowd followed the matches on the screen all evening" >}}

Gear and beer in good company on a Friday night. Does it get any better?
