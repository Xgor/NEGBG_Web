## The Final

{{< figure src="POV-youarewatchinggrandfinalsattheesportsbar.jpg" caption="POV: you're watching the semifinal at Kappa Bar" >}}

The final was supposed to be Jenxey vs Oskar, but Oskar had to DQ right after beating feffe, so grand finals ended up being Jenxey vs feffe instead.
Jenxey took it home with Sol and climbed from 2nd place last tournament to the top of the podium this time.
