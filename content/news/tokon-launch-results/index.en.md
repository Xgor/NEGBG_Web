Last week, on August 7, we at Negative Edge held our Marvel Tokon Fighting Souls release tournament at Kappa Bar, Rosenlundsgatan 8. It turned out to be a smaller, more casual event where we all got to try out the new game—which had been released the day before—figure out how the game’s mechanics work, and get to know our characters.
<!--more-->
{{< figure src="20260807_204756.jpg" >}}
{{< figure src="20260807_204308.jpg" caption="pictures of people playing" >}}

This was the first time we’ve hosted an event on a Friday at Kappa Bar, which was celebrating its 10th anniversary that evening, so there were quite a few people partying and karaoke going on. Because of that, we decided not to stream or record a VOD this time, since it would have clashed with everything else.
Thanks to everyone who showed up at the event, and congratulations to the players KongoBongo. Apos and Furby, who took 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place, respectively.
